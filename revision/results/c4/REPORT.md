# Concern 4 (4th review) — closed-loop FCT under reordering models between no penalty and the full penalty

Protocol: `PROTOCOL.md` (committed with the code before any run). Raw runs: `runs.jsonl` (2,160). Statistics:
`summary.json`, `tables.md` (generated by `revision/scripts/c4_analyze.py`). Gates: `gate.json`. Runtime: `runtime.json`.

## Summary
- **Gates:** all pass. 480 new runs equal the saved no-penalty and full-penalty rows field for field, and the Table A6
  statistics (means, p99s, percentages, CIs) are reproduced exactly from them.
- **Mean FCT, guarded score vs score without rules:** the guard's advantage is **detected at every tested setting**,
  from W = 0 (+28.3 ticks [+19.9, +35.1], +23.1%) to no penalty (+6.5 [+3.9, +8.9], +5.3%), and under `rtt/4` and
  dupACK 3. It shrinks as the window grows and levels off by W = 4–8. **No crossover can be established in the tested range
  for mean FCT** (W = 0 … 16 and ∞). The same holds against FlowBender-style, ECMP, reactive and unguarded GNN.
- **p99 FCT, guarded score vs score without rules:** detected **only at W = 0** (and dupACK 3). It is not detected at
  any W ≥ 1, under `rtt/4`, or with no penalty. Verdicts are monotone, so the crossover bracket is **(0, 1]**: tolerating a
  displacement of one tick already removes the detectable p99 advantage.
- **No setting** has any comparator detectably better than guarded score (no CI entirely below zero), for mean or p99.
- **dupACK 3 ≈ full penalty in this simulator:** 97.7% of reordered batches carry ≥ 3 packets (median 54). A dupACK count
  below 3 occurs for 0.5% of reordered batches, so the rule penalises 99.3% of them (W = 0: 100%).

**Wording the evidence supports.** On mean FCT, guarded score's advantage over score without rules, FlowBender-style
and ECMP survives every reordering tolerance tested, from none to 16 ticks, the RACK-like `rtt/4` window and a 3-dupACK
threshold. The advantage is about four times larger when every reordered batch is penalised. On p99 FCT, the advantage
over score without rules is detected only when every reordered batch is penalised (W = 0 or dupACK 3); one tick of
tolerance removes it. In this simulator, dupACK 3 is not a middle case: it is almost the full penalty, because reordered
batches carry tens of packets. These are single-topology, tick-model results (k = 4 Fat-Tree, 30 cells, batch-level
penalty).

+5.31% [+3.57, +7.25], which is detected; this study reproduces that number exactly. The p99 contrast there (−0.8%) does
include 0. The paper was not checked or changed.
